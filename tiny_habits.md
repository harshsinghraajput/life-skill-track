# Tiny Habits

## Question 1
The most interesting idea was that **small habits can create big changes** when repeated consistently. Starting with a very small action makes it easier to continue.

## Question 2
**B = MAP** means:

- **M – Motivation:** The desire to do the habit.
- **A – Ability:** Make the habit simple and easy to do.
- **P – Prompt:** A reminder or trigger that starts the habit.

Making a habit tiny reduces the effort required and makes it easier to repeat.

## Question 3
Celebrating after completing a habit creates a **positive feeling**. This helps the brain associate the action with something rewarding, making the habit more likely to happen again.

## Question 4
The most interesting idea was the **1% improvement principle**. Small improvements every day can compound into significant progress over time.

## Question 5
*Atomic Habits* emphasizes **identity-based habits**. Instead of focusing only on what you want to achieve, focus on the person you want to become. Your repeated actions reinforce that identity.

## Question 6
To make a habit easier:

- Make it obvious.
- Reduce the number of steps.
- Prepare everything in advance.
- Start with a very small version of the habit.
- Make the environment support the habit.

## Question 7
To make a bad habit harder:

- Make the cue invisible.
- Add friction to the behavior.
- Remove easy access.
- Create extra steps before doing it.
- Use barriers or restrictions.

## Question 8
**Habit: Practice coding daily**

Steps:
- Keep my laptop and study material ready.
- Set a fixed time for coding.
- Start with just 10 minutes.
- Begin with one simple problem.
- Celebrate after completing the session.

## Question 9
**Habit to reduce: Excessive social media**

Steps:
- Remove social media apps from the home screen.
- Turn off notifications.
- Keep the phone away while studying.
- Set app usage limits.
- Make coding/studying the easier alternative.
