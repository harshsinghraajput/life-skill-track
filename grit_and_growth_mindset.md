# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

**Answer:** Grit means staying committed to a goal and continuing to work hard even when things become difficult.

## Question 2

**Answer:** A growth mindset means believing that skills and intelligence can improve through effort, learning, and practice.

## Question 3

**Answer:** Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and choices can influence our results. The key point is to focus on what we can control instead of blaming outside factors.

## Question 4

**Answer:**
- Embrace challenges.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Put in consistent effort.
- Accept feedback.
- Learn from others.
- Stay positive during difficulties.
- Keep improving.

## Question 5

**Answer:**
- Treat mistakes as learning opportunities.
- Practice consistently.
- Ask for and accept feedback.
- Set small learning goals.
- Focus on progress, not perfection.
- Take responsibility for my actions.
