## Question 4
**Sleep is Your Superpower — Key Points**

- Sleep is essential for physical and mental health.
- Good sleep improves memory and learning.
- Sleep helps the brain process and store information.
- Lack of sleep affects concentration and decision-making.
- Poor sleep can negatively affect health.
- Consistent, sufficient sleep improves daily performance.

## Question 5
**Ideas to sleep better**

- Follow a fixed sleep schedule.
- Avoid screens before sleeping.
- Avoid caffeine late in the day.
- Keep the bedroom quiet and comfortable.
- Exercise regularly.
- Relax before going to bed.

## Question 6
**Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise — Key Points**

- Exercise improves brain function.
- It improves memory and learning.
- Exercise can reduce stress and anxiety.
- It improves mood and mental well-being.
- Exercise supports the growth and health of brain cells.
- Regular exercise can improve focus and concentration.

## Question 7
**Steps to exercise more**

- Set a fixed time for exercise.
- Start with 20–30 minutes daily.
- Walk or cycle instead of using transport for short distances.
- Take short movement breaks while working.
- Play a sport or do an activity I enjoy.
- Gradually increase the intensity and duration.
