## Question 3

**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

### 1. Active Mode 

>> Its like being focused on certain topic and learning in a way how our brain directly signals to do like normal way.
>> Sometimes we get stuck in normal problem because we are too much stress and some minor error is not visible.
Example : If you stuck in any Data Structures problem so we go with the same ancestral way by which it can be solved , not thinking that there could be another way of solving which can be easier and precise as well.

### 2.Diffused Mode 

>> Its sometimes quite good when we relax our state of mind we think out of box.
>> Relaxation helps us to think out of box and also helps us to know what problems we are getting stuck which was ignored previously. 
Example : When we get to know a 20 lines of code to solve a Data structure problem can be solved with in 5 to 8 lines this is when we relaxed and got to know more ways to solve the problem

## Question 4

**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

**Deconstruct the skill** — Know what you are going to learn.
**Learn enough to self-correct** — Notice your problem and correct it to solve it by different approach.
**Remove practice barriers** — Remove obstacles which makes you stop to solve the problem.
**Practice for at least 20 hours** — Consistency and focus is key to acheive your goal.
