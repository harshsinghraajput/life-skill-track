 Question 1

### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)

- Pay attention to the topic and the speaker instead of getting distracted.
- Let them finish before responding.
- Use open-ended questions to show interest in the conversation.
- Show that you’re listening through your body language.
- Take notes during important conversations.
- Paraphrase to show that you both are on the same page.

---

## Question 2

### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)

In simple words, Fisher's model explains how we should listen to the speaker and what mistakes we should avoid. If we understand the topic, we should respond appropriately. If something is unclear, we should ask for clarification and rephrase what the speaker said in our own words to make sure we understood it correctly.

## Question 3

### What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- **Distraction** - Be focused on the topic rather than diverting your mind.
- **Too quick to judge** - Without letting the other person complete the topic, we may come to a conclusion too quickly.
- **Overthinking** - Sometimes there is a simple answer, but we still overthink the situation and follow an approach that may not be correct.

---

## Question 4

### What can you do to improve your listening?

- **Avoid Distraction** - If I know I have a bad habit of getting distracted by phone notifications, I can keep my phone aside for a while.
- **Patience** - Let the speaker finish the topic before reaching a conclusion instead of assuming what they are going to say.
- **Avoid Interruption** - Don't break someone's flow while they are explaining something. Let them finish and then ask questions.

### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

If someone is speaking aggressively and I want to avoid a conflict because of our different opinions, I try to use a passive communication style.

### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

## Question 7

### When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

When I feel hurt or angry, I sometimes give the person silent treatment so that my aggression does not negatively affect their mental well-being. However, if the situation continues for too long and my frustration becomes too much, I may switch to an aggressive communication style.

## Question 8

### How can you make your communication assertive?

A few steps I can apply in my own life are:

- **Think before I speak** - I will take a moment to understand what I want to say instead of reacting emotionally.
- **Learn to say no** - I will say no when I cannot do something instead of agreeing and then feeling frustrated.
- **Control my tone and body language** - I will try to maintain a calm tone, eye contact, and confident body language instead of becoming aggressive.
- **Address problems directly** - Instead of using sarcasm, taunts, or silent treatment, I will discuss the problem openly and try to find a solution.
- **Stay calm during disagreements** - Even when I disagree with someone, I can be firm about my opinion without attacking the person.
